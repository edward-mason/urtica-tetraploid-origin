# AI DISCLOSURE (bioRxiv policy on generative AI)

**Tools.** Claude Science (Anthropic), an agentic research environment powered by the large language model Claude (model identifier claude-fable-5-1), used between 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-24 on the author's Windows 11 workstation. Sub-agents of the same system performed the literature inventory, the public-data inventory, an independent reproduction (Reviewer A) and a hostile review (Reviewer B) of the result.

**What the AI produced.** The AI generated the research plan, the hypothesis list and scoring, the preregistration text (including thresholds), all analysis code (Python, BLAST+ invocations, Snakemake workflow), all figures, the laboratory notebook, the literature map, the claim ledger, the validation plan and the full first draft of this manuscript, including the abstract, results, discussion, methods and reference list. Numerical results were produced by code written and executed by the AI on public data; reference metadata were retrieved programmatically from CrossRef and every DOI was machine-verified to resolve.

**What the human author did.** The human author defined the mission, constraints and quality criteria, approved the plan, granted data-access permissions, and is responsible for reading, checking and, where necessary, correcting every statement before submission. The human author is the sole author of record and takes full responsibility for the content; generative AI is not an author and does not meet authorship criteria.

**Known limitations of AI involvement.** Literature coverage depends on programmatic database queries (OpenAlex, CrossRef, Europe PMC, Semantic Scholar, bioRxiv) and may miss non-indexed theses or grey literature; the sub-agent literature report explicitly declared that a thesis search was not performed and that several older sources were characterised from abstracts only. The AI's interpretation of published tables (e.g., centromere interval tables) was mechanical and is documented in the notebook. Text passages were written by the AI from the numerical results and should be read as such.

**Independent review inside the system.** Two sub-agents with fresh contexts, no access to the draft manuscript and separate working directories reviewed the work (paper/reviews/): Reviewer A reproduced the headline statistics from archived tables and from new BLAST searches; Reviewer B performed a hostile review and its own recomputations. Their reports are supplied verbatim and every point and the action taken is listed in paper/REVIEWER_RESPONSES.md. The mission brief also asked for comparative-plant-genomics criteria to be added to the platform's Reviewer specialist (Settings → Specialists → Reviewer); that is a user-interface setting the AI cannot change and was not done — the reviewer agents were instead given explicit domain criteria in their task text.

**What the AI did not do.** It did not download or analyse raw sequencing reads, run flow cytometry, or access any non-public data; it did not read the full text of Rejlová et al. 2021, Geltman 1990 or the Rejlová PhD thesis (closed access / not online), which are cited from abstracts or through secondary sources, as stated in the text. All web-search results used for the novelty check are listed in notes/notebook.md (2026-10-24).

**Human verification required before submission.** Author name and affiliation; confirmation that every cited quotation matches the source; a read of the full Rejlová et al. 2021 paper for any auto/allo analysis; optional execution of VALIDATION.md item 5 (k-mer analysis of the DToL reads), which would materially strengthen or overturn Claim 1.
