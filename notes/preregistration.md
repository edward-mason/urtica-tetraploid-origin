Common gene-space divergence pipeline (used by H1 and H3):
1. Queries: all RefSeq protein-coding CDS of MH2 (longest isoform per gene, CDS length ≥ 300 bp).
2. blastn (BLAST+ 2.17.0, task blastn, evalue 1e-20, outfmt with qseq/sseq) of each CDS against each assembly (FH1, FH2, MH1, MH2, DP, DA, UU).
3. Per target, HSPs are clustered into loci (same subject sequence, ≤ 50 kb apart, consistent strand). A gene is retained only if, in EVERY one of FH1, FH2, MH1, MH2, DP: exactly one locus has bitscore ≥ 0.8 × best locus bitscore (single-copy criterion) and the best locus covers ≥ 80 % of CDS length. For DA and UU the gene is used where it passes the same criterion but their absence does not exclude the gene from the H1 primary test.
4. A query-anchored alignment is built per gene: for each target, the base aligned to each CDS position (deletions as '-', target insertions dropped). Pairwise p-distance between any two targets = mismatches / columns where both have a base (requires ≥ 300 such columns).
5. Distances recorded per gene: d(FH1,FH2) = W; d(PAT,FH1), d(PAT,FH2); d(DP,FH1), d(DP,FH2), d(DP,PAT); d(DA, ·); d(DP,DA); d(·,UU); d(MAT,FH_source) as technical floor.
Expected sample: ≥ 10,000 genes; the test is declared underpowered if < 2,000 genes pass.

## H1 (primary). Origin of the tetraploid cytotype: same-lineage (auto)tetraploid vs allotetraploid
**Hypothesis.** The tetraploid U. dioica genome sampled by DToL derives from the same diploid lineage as the Romanian/Bulgarian diploids (autotetraploid or same-lineage tetraploid), not from hybridisation with a divergent second lineage.
**Statistics.** For each gene: baseline b = min(d(PAT,FH1), d(PAT,FH2)) — divergence of an unrelated diploid haplotype (Bulgaria) from the nearest of the two haplotypes of the Romanian female; test t = min(d(DP,FH1), d(DP,FH2)) — the same quantity for the tetraploid pseudo-haplotype. Both use the same two reference haplotypes, so the min-of-two bias is identical. R = median(t)/median(b). f99 = fraction of genes with t > Q99(b); f95 analogous. Spatial clustering of the indicator [t > Q99(b)] along MH2 gene order: Moran's I at lag 1 compared with 1,000 within-chromosome circular shifts. Bootstrap CIs (1,000 resamples of 2-Mb gene blocks) for R, f95, f99. Secondary: distribution of d(DP,DA) vs W and b (bimodality: Hartigan's dip test p < 0.01 plus a second mode above Q99(b)).
**Predictions and decision rule (fixed now).**
- Same-lineage tetraploid: 0.67 ≤ R ≤ 1.5 AND f99 ≤ 0.05 AND no second mode in d(DP,DA).
- Allotetraploid, one subgenome from the sampled diploid lineage: f99 ≥ 0.20 AND the high-t genes are spatially clustered (Moran's I above the 99th percentile of the null) AND d(DP,DA) bimodal.
- Allotetraploid, both subgenomes divergent: R ≥ 2 AND f99 ≥ 0.50.
- Any other combination: inconclusive; reported as such.
**Falsifiers.** H1 is falsified by either allotetraploid outcome. The same-lineage verdict is itself falsified if the held-out replicate (below) disagrees.
**Null / negative controls.** (i) Technical floor: median d(MAT,FH_source) must be < 0.1 × median(W) outside recombined intervals; otherwise the pipeline's error rate is too high and the test is void. (ii) Positive scale control: median d(·,UU) must exceed median(b) by ≥ 3×; otherwise the assay lacks dynamic range. (iii) Within-individual W vs between-population b are reported to show the assay resolves population structure.
**Held-out dataset.** DA (GCA_964188125.1) is not examined until the DP analysis is finalised and written to the notebook. The test is repeated with DA in place of DP; the result is reported as confirmed only if the verdict is identical.
**Artifact checks (to be run and reported).** Collapsed homoeologs in DP (genes with a second DP locus ≥ 97 % identity and ≥ 80 % coverage — their count and the t distribution with/without them); DP unplaced scaffolds vs chromosomes; chromosome-8 inversion interval (results with chr8 excluded); per-chromosome R to detect misassembly/chimeric pseudomolecules; CDS-only design removes TE/GC confounding; contamination: genes whose best DP hit is on a scaffold < 100 kb are reported separately.
**Disk/runtime estimate.** Downloads ≈ 4.5 GB compressed assemblies + 0.4 GB annotations; BLAST DBs ≈ 4 GB; peak < 15 GB. blastn of ~28 Mb of CDS against 7 genomes ≈ 1–3 h total on 8 threads. Well below both ceilings.

## H2 (secondary). Direction of the chr8/chr13 (U. dioica) – chr2 (U. urens) karyotype change
**Hypothesis.** The difference is a fission in the U. dioica lineage (ancestral state = one chromosome), rather than a fusion in the U. urens lineage.
**Test.** Reciprocal best BLASTP hits (evalue 1e-10, ≥ 50 % mutual coverage) between MH2 RefSeq proteins and each of UU, Parietaria, ramie (GWH proteins if retrievable, else tblastn-based), Morus notabilis, Cannabis cs10. Collinear blocks: ≥ 5 RBH pairs within a sliding window of 20 consecutive genes on both genomes. Define segment S8 = MH2 chr8 genes in blocks with UU chr2, S13 = MH2 chr13 genes in blocks with UU chr2. For each outgroup, compute the fraction of S8 and S13 block-genes whose orthologs lie on a single shared outgroup chromosome.
**Decision rule.** Fission in U. dioica is supported if, in ≥ 2 outgroups including ≥ 1 of Parietaria/ramie, ≥ 70 % of S8 and ≥ 70 % of S13 block-genes lie on the SAME outgroup chromosome. Fusion in U. urens is supported if, in ≥ 2 outgroups including ≥ 1 of Parietaria/ramie, ≥ 70 % of S8 and ≥ 70 % of S13 lie on DIFFERENT outgroup chromosomes. Otherwise inconclusive (independent rearrangement in outgroups).
**Falsifier.** The fusion outcome falsifies H2.
**Controls.** (i) Negative control: a randomly chosen pair of U. dioica chromosomes not involved (drawn now by seed 20261023 from chromosomes 1–7, 9–12) must map to different outgroup chromosomes with the same rule — demonstrates the test can return "separate". (ii) Supporting, not decisive: interstitial telomeric repeats (TTTAGGG)n, n ≥ 4, within 1 Mb of the S8/S13 junction on UU chr2; terminal telomere arrays at the centromere-proximal ends of chr8 and chr13.
**Artifact checks.** Chr8 inversion (blocks defined on both FH1 and MH2 orientations); annotation completeness differences (report block-gene counts per outgroup); paralog contamination (RBH only).
**Disk/runtime.** Proteomes < 0.2 GB; BLASTP all pairs ≈ 1–2 h; tblastn of 23k proteins vs ramie genome ≤ 6 h if needed.

## H3 (tertiary). Diversity landscape differs between predicted acrocentric and polycentric chromosomes
**Hypothesis.** Predicted acrocentric chromosomes (8, 9, 11, 12, 13) show a pericentromeric trough in nucleotide diversity; predicted polycentric chromosomes (1–7; chr10 "metacentric" treated as its own class and reported separately) do not.
**Measure.** Per gene, π̂ = mean of d(FH1,FH2), d(PAT,FH1), d(PAT,FH2) (three diploid haplotypes). Gene position = FH1 coordinate of the FH1 locus. Centromere position per chromosome from Hirabayashi 2025 Table S8 (acrocentric: the predicted point; polycentric: midpoint of the predicted diffuse region).
**Statistics.** Per chromosome: Spearman ρ between π̂ and distance to predicted centromere; trough ratio T = median π̂ of the 20 % of genes nearest the centromere / median π̂ of the remaining 80 %. Null for ρ: 1,000 circular shifts of gene positions along the chromosome. Class comparison: difference in mean T between the 5 acrocentric and 7 polycentric chromosomes, exact permutation over all C(12,5) = 792 label assignments (chr10 excluded from the class test, reported alone).
**Decision rule.** H3 supported if ρ > 0 with shift-null p < 0.05 in ≥ 4 of 5 acrocentric chromosomes, AND mean T(acro) < 0.8, AND exact permutation p < 0.05 for T(acro) < T(poly). Falsified if ≤ 2 acrocentric chromosomes show the gradient or if polycentric chromosomes show the same mean T (permutation p > 0.2). Otherwise inconclusive.
**Confound control (fixed now).** Because the acrocentric centromere lies near one end, distance-to-centromere correlates with distance-to-that-end. On polycentric chromosomes the same statistic is computed relative to the nearest chromosome end; if diversity rises toward ends on polycentric chromosomes with comparable ρ, the acrocentric gradient is attributed to a telomere effect and H3 is NOT claimed. The chr8 inversion interval (FH1 coordinates from the synteny analysis) is masked.
**Held-out.** Male assembly: π̂_male = d(MH1,MH2) per gene with male centromere predictions; not examined until the female-based analysis is written up. Confirmed only if the verdict is identical.
**Artifact checks.** Gene-density circularity (Table S8 positions coincide with gene-density minima: report results with the 2 Mb around each predicted centromere removed); assembly gaps (N-runs) near centromeres; collapsed regions (genes failing single-copy filter are counted per window).
