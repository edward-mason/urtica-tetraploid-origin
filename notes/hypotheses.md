# Hypotheses — Urtica dioica comparative genomics
Written 2026-10-23T08:32:48+01:00, before any genome sequence was downloaded. Scores 1–5: N = novelty (judged against notes/literature.md), T = testability with data in hand, S = significance, A = artifact risk (5 = lowest risk), F = feasibility under Windows-native, no-WSL, 80 GB / 72 h limits. "Racing" = is this the obvious next paper for the data-generating group (UBC/Cronk–Hirabayashi; DToL/Kew; Bonn)?

## Shortlist (no-racing rule applied)
1. **H1 — tetraploid origin** (primary). Highest novelty and significance; a crisp two-sided prediction; feasible with BLAST + Python; the DToL alternate haplotype is a natural held-out replicate.
2. **H2 — direction of the chr8/chr13 fission/fusion** (secondary). Modest, concrete, uses outgroups nobody has used; shares the synteny machinery with H1's chromosome checks.
3. **H3 — diversity landscape vs centromere class** (tertiary). Exploits the polycentric/acrocentric natural experiment; reuses H1's per-gene distances; H4/H5/H12 are follow-ups if H3 is informative.
Excluded as headline: H7, H14, H18 (racing/SDR); H9, H10 (annotation heterogeneity, GPU-free but tree-inference-limited); H16 (data not in hand).
